Description

Tilefall is a roguelike puzzle game where every tile you step off drops into the void. Each run deals you a new, randomly generated board: plan your route, grab power-ups on the way and don't strand yourself on a shrinking island. Runs last 30 seconds to a minute, made for one more try. The first 2 worlds are free; the full game has 10+, each harder than the last and with its own look.

History

Tilefall is the first game from piotrsowiak.pl, an indie studio from Łódź, Poland, founded by Piotr Sowiak, a .NET/JS developer. Development started on 19 October 2026.

The game is written in TypeScript on HTML5 Canvas and wrapped for Android with Capacitor, so the browser and Android versions share one codebase. The browser version launches first, on 28 November 2026, followed by Google Play on 12 December 2026.

Features

  • The floor falls away behind you: every tile you leave drops into the void.
  • A new board every run: layouts are randomly generated, so there's no solution to memorise.
  • Power-ups to collect during each run.
  • 10+ worlds, each harder than the last and with its own visual theme.
  • Short runs: 30 seconds to a minute.
  • Free with no ads; a one-time $3.99 purchase unlocks the full game. The first 2 worlds are free; the purchase unlocks all 10+.
  • Plays in the browser and on Android. Controls: move one tile at a time: swipe on a touchscreen, or use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard.

Tilefall — privacy policy

Last updated: {PRIVACY_DATA}

This policy describes what data the game Tilefall processes: the Android app on Google Play and the version you play in a browser. The website, newsletter and email contact are covered by the piotrsowiak.pl privacy policy.

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The one-time unlock is paid through Google Play; I never see your payment details.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Piotr Sowiak, Łódź, Poland, the developer of Tilefall. For anything related to privacy, write to kontakt@piotrsowiak.pl.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your progress, settings and whether you've unlocked the full game in the app's local storage (in the browser version: the browser's local storage). This data isn't sent to me. It's deleted when you uninstall the app, clear the app's data or clear the site data in your browser.

Purchases

The full game is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. I don't receive your card or payment details. Google gives me order information needed to deliver the purchase, handle refunds and meet tax obligations.

  • Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations such as tax and accounting rules (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Retention: until tax liabilities expire under Polish law, usually five years from the end of the year in which the tax was due.

Ratings and reviews

The game may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review window. Google handles that window and your rating; I only see public reviews, the same way everyone else does on Google Play.

Android permissions

The app uses only one permission: internet access (INTERNET), a standard permission of the app framework. The game doesn't use it to send your data anywhere.

Analytics and crash reports

The game doesn't use analytics or crash reporting tools.

Browser version

The browser version is published on itch.io. When you play it there, itch.io's own privacy policy applies to their website. The game itself works the same way as on Android: your progress stays in your browser.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Who else receives data

  • Google — distribution through Google Play, payments, in-app reviews and app statistics that Google collects on its own (for example installs and crash statistics shared by your device).
  • itch.io — if you play the browser version there.

I don't sell personal data and don't share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

The game has no accounts, so there's no account to delete. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To ask about any other data, write to kontakt@piotrsowiak.pl.

Security

The game collects as little data as possible. Data handled by me is accessed only by me, and the website and downloads are served over HTTPS.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format and object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl). Details are in the piotrsowiak.pl privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when the game gets a new feature that processes data, I'll update this page and change the date at the top.

FAQ

When can I play?

In your browser from Saturday 28 November 2026. On Google Play from Saturday 12 December 2026, with pre-registration open from 16 November.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free and has no ads. A one-time purchase of $3.99 (local prices on Google Play) unlocks the full game. The first 2 worlds are free; the purchase unlocks all 10+.

How long is a run?

30 seconds to a minute. It's built for quick sessions and "just one more try".

How do I control it?

Move one tile at a time: swipe on a touchscreen, or use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard.

Who makes it?

One person: Piotr Sowiak, a .NET/JS developer from Łódź, Poland, building the game after hours. It's written in TypeScript on HTML5 Canvas and wrapped for Android with Capacitor, so the web and Android versions share one codebase.

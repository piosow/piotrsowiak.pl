Free add-on for Google Sheets™

Your schedule is in a spreadsheet. Get it into calendars.

SheetRota turns rows with dates and times (shift rotas, project deadlines, room bookings) into calendar events. Check the rows, then download one .ics file for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, or add events one by one.

Coming soon to Google Workspace Marketplace How it works

No account, no sign-up. The add-on only reads the spreadsheet you open it in.

How it works

1

Open it in your sheet

Extensions → SheetRota → Open. Columns like Date, Start, End and Title are found automatically. No sheet yet? Insert the example schedule.

2

Check the rows

Every row gets a ✓ or a plain-language note: an unreadable date, a missing title, an unknown time zone. Fix it in the sheet and check again.

3

Export

Download one .ics file and import it into any calendar, or click “Add to Google Calendar” next to a single row.

Gets the details right

  • Time zones per row, with summer and winter time
  • Night shifts: 22:00–06:00 ends the next morning
  • All-day events when there's no start time
  • Date cells, 2026-11-03 or 03.11.2026
  • English and Polish column headers
  • Up to 2,000 rows per export

Your data stays in your spreadsheet

SheetRota asks for two permissions: to read the spreadsheet it's opened in and to show its sidebar. It can't see your other files or your calendar. The .ics file is built and downloaded in your browser. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else, and the add-on has no tracking. Details in the privacy policy.

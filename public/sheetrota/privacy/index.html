Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers the SheetRota add-on for Google Sheets, its website at piotrsowiak.pl/sheetrota/ and the SheetRota Pro waitlist.

  • The add-on reads only the spreadsheet you open it in, and only when you use it.
  • Your spreadsheet data is never sent to me or stored anywhere outside your Google account and your browser.
  • The add-on has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • I don't sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who is responsible for your data

SheetRota is made by Piotr Sowiak, {DANE_FIRMY}, Poland, who is the data controller for the data described below. For anything related to privacy, write to kontakt@piotrsowiak.pl.

The add-on: what Google data it uses and why

SheetRota asks for two Google permissions:

  • View and manage spreadsheets that this application has been installed in (spreadsheets.currentonly). SheetRota reads the header row and the rows you choose in the spreadsheet you opened it in, to turn them into calendar events and show you which rows need fixing. When you click “Insert example schedule”, it adds a new sheet with sample rows to that spreadsheet. It can't open any other file.
  • Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications (script.container.ui), to show the SheetRota sidebar.

SheetRota doesn't ask for access to your Google Calendar, Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or email address.

The add-on runs as a Google Apps Script inside your Google account. The rows are processed there and in your browser, and the .ics file is created and downloaded in your browser. None of this data is sent to servers run by me or by anyone else, and the add-on doesn't keep a copy after you close the sidebar. I can't see your spreadsheets.

When you click “Add to Google Calendar” next to a row, your browser opens Google Calendar with that event's details in the address, and you decide whether to save it. That happens directly between you and Google.

If the add-on fails, Google Cloud records a technical error report (the error message and where in the code it happened) in the add-on's project, without the contents of your spreadsheet. These reports are kept for 30 days and used only to fix bugs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SheetRota's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your spreadsheet is used only to provide the add-on's features you see in the sidebar. It isn't transferred to anyone, isn't used for advertising, isn't read by people and isn't used to train AI models.

Marketplace statistics

Google Workspace Marketplace shows me aggregated statistics for the SheetRota listing, such as the number of listing views and installs. I've linked the listing to Google Analytics to see the same kind of aggregate numbers. I don't receive the names or email addresses of people who install the add-on.

This website

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks (my legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; see the Cloudflare privacy policy). The website itself doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics.

SheetRota Pro waitlist

If you join the waitlist, I collect what you enter in the form: your email address, team size, the tool you use today and an optional comment, together with where you came from (for example “from the add-on”) and the form language.

  • Purpose: to tell you when SheetRota Pro is available and offer you the launch discount, and to send you one short survey about your needs. No other marketing.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by replying to any email or writing to kontakt@piotrsowiak.pl.
  • Retention: until you withdraw consent, and no longer than 12 months after SheetRota Pro launches or is cancelled.
  • The form is provided by Tally BV (Belgium), which stores the answers on my behalf in the EU. See the Tally privacy policy.

Emailing me

If you write to me for help, I process your email address and your message to reply (my legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Email is hosted by Zoho in its EU data centre. I keep support emails for two years after the last message in a conversation.

Who else receives data

  • Google — runs the add-on in your account and provides Marketplace statistics.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security.
  • Tally — waitlist form.
  • Zoho — email (EU data centre).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this doesn't affect processing done before. Write to kontakt@piotrsowiak.pl and I'll respond within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

To remove the add-on, open Google Sheets → Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons → SheetRota → Uninstall. You can also review its access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

SheetRota isn't directed at children under 16. I don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Changes

If SheetRota starts using new data or permissions (for example, calendar access in SheetRota Pro), I'll update this policy before the change and ask Google for the new permissions, which you'll have to approve. The date at the top shows the latest version.
